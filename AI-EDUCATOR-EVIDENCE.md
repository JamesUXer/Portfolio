# AI Educator: evidence and publication boundaries

Updated 26 October 2026. This is an editorial record, not public page copy.

## Confirmed directly by James in this task

- The AI policy was completed and adopted. James developed the policy and training, pitched to senior leadership and rolled out training across staff.
- AI Educator formed part of that development. Beginner and intermediate users were in scope; advanced users were outside scope.
- Comparable surveys were completed at different points over one year by more than 50 staff. They support increased self-reported use, confidence and understanding of policy.
- Effective classroom application remains unproven. Do not attribute the survey changes to the digital module alone.
- Government advice and professional training informed content on personal data and hallucinations. The exact professional-training provider, course and dates remain unverified.
